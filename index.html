Curriculum

What You'll Learn

DAY 1
Audio Foundations & Signal Flow
Understanding Sound Physics — frequency, amplitude, wavelength
Signal chain fundamentals — mic to speaker pathway
Types of microphones and their polar patterns
Cables, connectors, and impedance matching
Introduction to mixing consoles — analogue & digital
Gain structure and headroom management
Understanding dB, SPL and metering
Basic troubleshooting of audio signal issues
DAY 2
EQ, Dynamics & Effects Processing
Parametric and graphic EQ — practical applications
Compressors, limiters, gates — how and when to use
Reverb and delay — types and creative use
Channel strips and insert/send/return routing
Mix bus processing and mastering chain basics
Digital effects units and plug-in equivalents
Practical mixing session — full band mix exercise
Ear training — identifying frequencies by ear
DAY 3
Live Sound & PA Systems
PA system design — line arrays, point source, subs
Stage setup, patch bay, and multicore cabling
FOH and Monitor mixing simultaneously
Feedback control and ring-out procedures
Wireless microphone systems — RF management
In-ear monitor (IEM) system setup and operation
Soundcheck workflow for live performances
Show day protocol and artist communication
DAY 4–6
Advanced Topics, Studio & Live Event Project
Digital Audio Workstations — recording & playback
Church sound engineering — unique challenges
Outdoor event audio — weather, distance, projection
Live event project — plan, setup, operate full show
Broadcast audio fundamentals
Business of audio — freelancing, contracts, pricing
Portfolio building and career roadmap
Certification examination and graduation

FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need any prior experience?

No experience is required. The course is designed to take complete beginners to job-ready engineers in 6 days. Passion and willingness to learn is all you need.

What is the course fee?

Please contact us via WhatsApp or the form below for current batch fees. We also offer early-bird discounts and payment plans for students.

Is the certificate recognized?

Yes. RUACH Institute certificates are recognized by event companies, churches, production houses and studios across India. Our alumni work in major productions.

What are the batch timings?

We run both weekday (Mon–Sat) and weekend batches to accommodate working professionals and college students. Contact us for the next available batch schedule.

Will I get job placement support?

Yes. We maintain a strong network of employers and actively refer our graduates for live event and church audio opportunities. Many students are hired within 2–4 weeks.

How many students per batch?

We cap each batch at 8–12 students to ensure personalized attention and maximum console time for every participant.
