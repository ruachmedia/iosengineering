FAQ

Common Questions

Do I Need Any Prior Experience?

No. This training is designed for both beginners and those with prior audio experience. We start with the fundamentals and gradually move into practical live sound applications.

What Will I Learn During The Training?

You will learn essential live sound engineering concepts including signal flow, gain structure, EQ, dynamics processing, feedback control, system setup, stage patching, and live mixing workflows.

Will I Receive Hands-On Practical Training?

Yes. This is a highly practical training program where students work directly with professional audio equipment and real-world sound engineering workflows.

How Can I Get Course Details & Fee Information?

For course fees, batch dates, seat availability, and registration details, please contact us directly through WhatsApp. Our team will be happy to assist you.

Will I Get Job Placement Support?

While we do not guarantee job placements, we do support students by sharing relevant opportunities whenever requirements arise from event companies, churches, production teams, or industry vendors. Students may also be included in our network for future recommendations and opportunities.

How Many Students Are Accepted Per Batch?

Each batch is limited to 20 students to ensure personalized guidance, better interaction, and maximum hands-on practical experience.

What Equipment Will I Train On?

Students will gain experience with professional digital consoles, microphones, audio processors, stage systems, and PA systems commonly used in live events and productions.

Who Can Join This Training?

This program is ideal for beginners, church sound operators, musicians, volunteers, aspiring sound engineers, and anyone interested in learning professional live sound engineering.

How Do I Register For The Training?

Simply contact us through WhatsApp, and our team will guide you through the registration process and help you secure your seat in the upcoming batch.
